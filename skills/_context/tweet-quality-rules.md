# Tweet Quality Rules

Use these rules when drafting or judging co-founder tweets.

## Grounding

- Every number, %, date, $ amount, ranking, proper-noun fact, and time-relative claim must come from supplied context, fact pack, or exemplars.
- Do not infer fresh stats from adjacent stats.
- If the current CT sentiment is skeptical, do not write a bullish hook unless the tweet earns that turn with proof.

## Argument

- Hook, body, and Immutable relevance must form one causal argument.
- The tweet should make a specific belief shift, not merely restate a market trend.
- If Immutable is mentioned, explain why it matters to the argument. Do not tack on a brand line.

## Voice

- Sound like an informed operator, not a marketing account.
- Avoid polished AI filler: "that volume", "exactly that scale", "steady, structural, compounding", "built for exactly this", and generic inevitability.
- Avoid abstract contrast scaffolding. If you make a contrast, both sides need concrete, falsifiable proof: a number, price, date, named game/studio/product, specific metric, or observable behavior. Do not write lines like "That is not a web3 pitch. It is basic cost structure." Prefer showing the contrast through facts, e.g. "Mobile stores charge 30%. Stablecoins charge ~1%."
- Avoid vague sentence-initial `That` or `This` as the subject of a claim. Name the noun instead. Bad: "That gap is..." Better: "The 30% fee gap is..." Relative-clause uses are fine, e.g. "the bear case is that only...".
- Avoid vague demonstrative references anywhere in the sentence. Do not write endings like "ends that", "proves that", "solves this", or "closes it" unless the noun is explicitly named.
- Avoid staccato abstract sentence chains outside hook-and-bullets format. Short sentences are only useful when they carry concrete proof. Bad: "The data layer was always the problem. UA budgets just masked it." Better: name the data layer, metric, channel, or cost.
- Do not use emojis, hashtags, em dashes, or corporate abstractions.
- Avoid these terms in final copy unless the user explicitly asked for them: crypto, IMX, NFT, blockchain.
- Do not mention Gods Unchained or use Gods Unchained data in any tweet.
- Do not use stale Immutable boilerplate stats such as "4 million Passport users" or "500+ live games". Prefer the latest supplied spreadsheet/fact-pack stats, and omit the metric if the current value is unavailable.

## Format

- Stay under 280 characters unless the requested format explicitly allows longer.
- Hookbullets format is exactly one hook line plus three concise `•` bullet lines. No closer.

## Archetype Rules

### Signing Preannouncement

This archetype is a narrow teaser format. It is not a product-stat tweet.

Allowed shapes:

1. `Immutable just signed a game with [bullish game/partner metric or proof point].`
2. `Tomorrow we announce a game with [bullish game/partner metric or proof point].`

Rules:

- Use the signed game, studio, genre, audience, distribution, revenue, player, wishlist, MAU, funding, pedigree, or franchise proof point.
- Do not mention Immutable product stats or generic platform claims: Passport, zkEVM, wallets, seed phrases, gas, Google login, attribution, 70% market share, game-count totals, or ecosystem-level traction.
- If the operator did not provide a game-specific proof point, write a conservative teaser around the existence of the signing only. Do not borrow generic Immutable facts to fill the gap.
- Keep it short. The point is anticipation, not explanation.
